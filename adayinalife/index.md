## How it works

- Tap **Add**, give the task a title and a description, and tap **Create Task**. From then on it has 24 hours.
- Choose up to two reminders, from 1 to 12 hours before the time runs out.
- Tap a task under **Tasks** to see the time left, and to complete it or give up on it.
- A task that isn't completed within 24 hours fails by itself.
- **Stats** shows how many tasks you completed and how many failed, filtered by status and dates.

A Day in a Life collects no data. Your tasks stay on your device. Read the [privacy policy](privacy/).
