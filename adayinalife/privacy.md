# A Day in a Life Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

A Day in a Life is a to-do list by BaerOak where every task has 24 hours. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. A Day in a Life does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data or usage data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

- **Your tasks**, with their titles, descriptions and whether you completed them, are saved on your device.
- **Your reminders** are scheduled on your device by iOS.
- Like other app data, these may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), according to your device's backup settings.

Deleting the app removes all of this from your device.

## Notifications

A Day in a Life asks for permission to send notifications only so it can remind you before a task's time runs out. You choose the reminders for each task, and you can turn notifications off in Settings at any time.

## Internet

This version of A Day in a Life does not connect to the internet.

## Earlier versions

Version 2.1 and earlier included Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, which sent Google anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. Later versions don't include them.

## Children

A Day in a Life does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
