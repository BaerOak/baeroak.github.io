# Stomme Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

Stomme is an app by BaerOak that keeps track of your home: its floor plan, renovations, upkeep and papers. It is designed to keep everything on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. Stomme does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data or usage data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

- **Your homes**: their floor plans, rooms, projects, upkeep and settings are saved on your device.
- **Documents and photos you add**, such as an energy declaration, receipts, manuals or a photo of an appliance's rating plate, are copied into the app's storage on your device. Their text is read on the device so you can search it.
- Like other app data, these may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), according to your device's backup settings.

Deleting the app removes all of this from your device.

## Camera

Stomme asks for camera access only so you can scan documents and photograph appliances' rating plates. Scans and photos stay on your device.

## Files from other apps

When you open or share a PDF, a picture or a Stomme backup into Stomme, the app copies it into its own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

## Sharing

Backups, quote requests and documents leave your device only when you choose to share them, using the iOS share sheet. What happens next is up to the app or service you share with.

## Maps

When you place your home on the map, Stomme uses Apple Maps: the map imagery, and the position of your address when you look it up, come from Apple, which receives the area shown and the address searched. Apple's privacy policy applies to that. The position you choose is saved only on your device.

## On-device intelligence

Reading text from pictures and answering questions about a manual happen on your device, using Apple's frameworks. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

## Notifications

If you turn on the Saturday reminders, they are planned on your device from your homes' upkeep. Nothing about them leaves your device.

## Children

Stomme does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
