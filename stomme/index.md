## How it works

- **Plan:** draw your home room by room, or read in a floor plan from a PDF or a picture. Tap a room to see it, tap it again to go in and add doors, windows, outlets and appliances.
- **Projects:** add a job, such as painting the walls of a room, and Stomme works out how much paint, flooring or skirting you need and roughly what it costs, with the ROT deduction. Share a quote request with a contractor as a PDF, or follow the steps when you do the job yourself.
- **Overview:** what's due in your house, its facts, its documents and appliances.
- **More:** back up a home to a file, which you can keep in Files or open on a new phone to restore it.

## Price

Stomme is free, with every feature, no ads and no in-app purchases.

Stomme collects no data. Your homes, documents and photos stay on your device. Read the [privacy policy](privacy/).
