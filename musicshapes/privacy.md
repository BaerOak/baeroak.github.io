# Music Shapes Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

Music Shapes is a music app by BaerOak that turns shapes into rhythms. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. Music Shapes does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data or usage data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

- **Your shapes and settings** are saved on your device so they're there next time you open the app.
- **Sounds you record or import** are copied into the app's storage on your device.
- Like other app data, these may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), according to your device's backup settings.

Deleting the app removes all of this from your device.

## Microphone

Music Shapes asks for microphone access only so you can record your own sounds. The microphone is used only while you are recording, and recordings stay on your device.

## Files from other apps

When you open or share an audio file into Music Shapes, the app copies it into its own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

## Sharing

Audio and video you export ("bounce") leave your device only when you choose to share them, using the iOS share sheet. What happens next is up to the app or service you share with.

## Internet

This version of Music Shapes does not connect to the internet.

## Children

Music Shapes does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
